What type of item are you reporting?

FindBack 2.0 uses two separate workflows to balance easy discovery for everyday items with total privacy for valuable electronics.

Public Workflow

Workflow A: Ordinary Items

For everyday personal possessions on the approved allowlist:

  • Water bottles, tumblers, and mugs
  • Notebooks, binders, and textbooks
  • Umbrellas and rain gear
  • Eyeglasses and sunglasses
  • Non-electronic stationery
  • Outerwear, jackets, scarves, and general tote bags

Public Listing: These items appear on the public campus board for easy claiming.

Confidential Workflow

Workflow B: High-Value Electronics

For electronic gadgets and high-value devices:

  • Smartphones, cellphones, and feature phones
  • Laptops and tablet computers
  • Wireless earbuds and headphones
  • Smartwatches and fitness trackers
  • Power adapters and portable chargers

Strictly Private: Electronics reports NEVER appear publicly. Matching is computed internally to prevent opportunistic theft.
